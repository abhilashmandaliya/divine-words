#### Poem

Words are just words … <br />
It’s Feelings Inward ! <br />
All so called relations of blood , <br />
It’s just temporary faces in clouds ! <br />
Someone is confused in Dates n Mate ! <br />
But just missing Mate Intimate ! <br />
All clouds , white n dark , going on … <br />
More than many times you have come n gone ! <br />
Awakened ones are ringing Bells … <br />
The Reality is … ONLY ONENESS PREVAILS !
